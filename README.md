## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Google Merchandise Store** dataset using **Google BigQuery (Standard SQL)** to solve real-world e-commerce business problems.

The objective is to transform raw website session data into actionable business insights through SQL analysis and build an interactive business intelligence dashboard using **Power BI**. The project demonstrates practical SQL skills commonly used by Data Analysts and Business Intelligence professionals.

# 📊 Key Business Insights

### 📦 Dataset

- Dataset contains over **21 million ecommerce session records**.
- Includes customer, marketing, product, and transaction information.
- Represents real-world Google Merchandise Store activity.

---

### 📢 Marketing Analysis

- Organic Search generates the highest website traffic.
- Referral visitors have the highest average page views.
- Display advertising contributes comparatively lower traffic.

---

### 🌍 Country Analysis

- Visitors originate from **215 countries**.
- The United States generates the highest website traffic.
- The United States contributes the highest revenue.
- A small number of countries contribute the majority of revenue.

---

### 🛍️ Product Analysis

- Identified the most viewed products on the website.
- Ranked products based on total revenue generated.
- Determined best-selling products by quantity sold.
- Evaluated product category performance.
- Identified high-performing product categories for business growth.

---

### 💰 Revenue Analysis

- Calculated overall store revenue.
- Identified the highest revenue-generating marketing channels.
- Ranked the top revenue-generating countries.
- Measured the average revenue per transaction.
- Identified the products contributing the highest revenue.

---

### 👥 Customer Analysis

- Measured the total number of unique visitors.
- Identified the countries contributing the largest customer base.
- Evaluated customer acquisition across marketing channels.
- Compared visitors who completed purchases with those who did not.
- Identified countries with the highest customer purchase rates.

### 📈 Conversion Rate Analysis
